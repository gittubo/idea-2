# Voice guide (placeholder)

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Our revolutionary new pack" | "Our new 28-litre pack" |
| "Built to last a lifetime!!" | "We'll repair it for as long as you own it." |
| "Elevate your commute" | "Your laptop stays dry in a downpour." |

- Subject lines: under 45 characters, no emoji, no all-caps.
- Sign-off: "— The Northpine team"
- Reading level: about grade 7. If a sentence needs a comma to breathe, split it.
