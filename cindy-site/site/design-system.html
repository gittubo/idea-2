design system

The Creative AI Notebook

Paper surfaces, small stickers and handwritten notes that decorate the page without competing with the content, one bold violet, and generous spacing so it stays easy to read. Every value below is a token at the top of styles.css, so a colour or size changes everywhere in one edit, including all the guides.

Colour

Electric violet does the talking: buttons, links, headings in guides, the club card. Lime and bubblegum are for stickers, highlights and tape, never for text.

Contrast (WCAG AA needs 4.5:1): violet on paper 4.9:1, white on violet 5.2:1, deep violet on paper 7.0:1, ink on lime 12.5:1, ink on bubblegum 9.7:1.
