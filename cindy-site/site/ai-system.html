Buy the lesson bundle once and keep ongoing access to its recorded lessons and guides. Your 30 days of Club support is separate from lesson access, so you can keep learning afterwards. Future bundles are offered separately.

How do the lessons work?

Your lessons are ready when you join. Choose a project, follow the recorded walkthrough and use the written guide as you build. Pause, try a step and come back whenever you need.

Can I join as a beginner?

Yes. Start with Claude 101, from setup and clear instructions to skills, MCPs and subagents. I’ll explain what each does and show you how to use it as we build. No coding background needed.

What if I get stuck?

You get 30 days of support from the Club team. Ask questions, share what you’re making and get help when you’re stuck.

Bring a laptop and choose the project you want to try. I’ll show you which tools you need for it and how to get set up. Some builds need a paid tool plan, such as Claude, which you’ll pay for separately. I’ll list those costs before you join, so you can plan for the builds you’re interested in.

When does it open?

Join the waitlist and I’ll email you as soon as enrolment opens. You’ll get first access and a look inside the lessons 🌱

Can I keep learning with you after the 30 days?

After your 30 days of Club support, you can choose to join AI Nook, our graduate community for ongoing learning and new lessons. It’s a separate, optional subscription. Your purchased lesson bundle stays accessible whether you join or not.

How much is it?

The waitlist is free, and waitlisters get the founding price. I’ll email you the lesson bundle price, payment options, lesson collection and refund details before enrolment opens.
