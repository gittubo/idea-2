Content creation Claude

Turn Claude into your viral content strategist

Hey, it's Cindy 🌱 Three steps: feed Claude the winners by link, turn the patterns into your own angles, then make it write in your voice.

Set the expectation first. This is a system and consistency, not a magic button. Claude does the research and writes the scripts. You still have to show up and film. What it removes is the blank page and the guesswork about what your niche actually rewards.
step one

Feed Claude the winners, by link

The mistake most people make is describing their competitors' hooks from memory. Instead, save the top-performing reels in your niche and hand Claude the links, then let it pull each one's transcript, hook, and structure for you. Paste ten or fifteen at once and ask for the pattern, not a single takeaway.

🔎 find the patterns 
Act as a viral content strategist for my niche (describe your niche + audience in one line).

Here are 10-15 top-performing reels in my space. For each one, break down: the exact hook (first line), the format/structure, the topic, and why it worked. Then zoom out and give me the patterns across all of them: which hook types, formats, and topics my niche is rewarding right now, ranked by how often they show up in the winners.
The achievability catch. Claude cannot watch an Instagram video on its own, so it needs the words. The clean way is a small reusable "analyze" skill (built once, the same way you would build any Claude skill) that takes a reel link and returns its transcript, hook, and structure, so you paste links, not text. No skill set up yet? Paste each reel's transcript or captions under the prompt above. Same analysis, one extra step.
step two

Turn the patterns into your angles

This is the step that keeps you original. You are not copying anyone's video, you are taking the structure that works and mapping it onto your own topics and point of view.

🧭 map winners onto your topics 
Here are the top 3 winning patterns you just found, and here are 10 topics I actually know and care about (list them).

For each of my topics, map it onto the strongest matching pattern and give me a fresh angle: a scroll-stopping hook in that structure, plus a one-line promise of what the viewer learns. Keep it in my world, don't reuse anyone else's example. Flag any topic that doesn't fit a proven pattern so I can drop it.
step three

Make it write in your voice

Generic AI scripts sound generic. Fix that once by building a voice skill from your own best posts, then reuse it forever. Use skill-creator, Anthropic's official skill for making skills, or just have Claude write the skill by hand.

🎙️ build your voice skill 
Here are 5 of my best-performing captions/scripts. Study my voice: sentence length, how casual I am, the words and phrases I actually use, how I open and close.

Use skill-creator to build a skill called "myvoice" that rewrites or drafts any script so it sounds like me, not default AI. It should keep the meaning and structure I give it and only change the voice. Save it so I can reuse it.

Then generate the batch, running your new voice skill over the angles from step two:

📝 generate 30 scripts 
Using /myvoice and the angles we mapped in step two, write me 30 short-form scripts.

Each one: a scroll-stopping hook as the first line, a tight body that teaches or shows one real thing, and a clear call to action. Keep them under ~60 seconds of spoken words. Number them and label each with its topic and the pattern it's built on.
what the pass extracts

The 4 things to read off every winner

The hook The exact first line. This is the single biggest driver of whether a video gets watched, so collect these obsessively.
The structure How the video is built: listicle, story, myth-bust, demo. The skeleton you can reuse on your own topic.
The topic What it is actually about, so you can see which subjects your niche is rewarding right now.
Why it worked The mechanism: curiosity gap, a bold number, a relatable pain. Naming it is how you repeat it on purpose.
The whole point is leverage: research once, build the voice skill once, then every future idea flows through a system instead of a blank page. Consistency is what compounds, and this is what makes consistency survivable.
