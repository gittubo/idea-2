the outcome

What you're making

A full set of professional headshots, LinkedIn profile photos, team-page shots, speaker photos, generated from a folder of your own pictures. Soul 2.0 locks your real face, so they're genuinely you, in clean lighting and a few outfits, without booking a photographer. Here are real examples:

Every one of these is me, generated from a folder of my own photos. No camera, no studio, no photographer.

step 1

Connect Higgsfield to Claude

  1. 🧩 In Claude, go to Customize, then Connectors
  2. ➕ Add a custom connector
  3. 🔗 Paste this URL: https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp, then Connect and approve the sign-in
This works on free Claude too. You're allowed one custom connector, and you may need to toggle it on per chat (the + next to the message box, then Connectors).
step 2

Open Claude Code and point it at your photos

So Claude can see your pictures, use Claude Code and start a new chat in the folder where your photos live (put 10 to 20 clear photos of yourself in one folder, say a folder called me). A few clean, front-on photos help the most.

step 3

Make a character of yourself

Ask Claude to build your character with Higgsfield's Soul 2.0, pointed at that folder:

🧬 create your character 
Using the Higgsfield MCP, create a Soul 2.0 character of me from the photos in this folder [point to your folder]. Keep it photoreal and true to my face.
step 4

Grab a few headshots you like on Pinterest

While it builds your character, save 3 to 6 corporate headshots or LinkedIn photos you like, note the lighting, the background, the pose, the crop.

step 5

Get your headshot set

Paste the references in and ask Claude to make them as you:

🪄 the headshots 
Give me these headshots, but as me, using my character. Clean professional lighting, neutral background, and a few different outfits. Give me the full set.

Claude renders a full set of professional headshots in one go. Ask for a different background or outfit and it keeps going.

no pinterest needed

Turn any photo into a prompt

You don't have to start on Pinterest. If you already have a shot you love (a reference, or even one Soul just made you), have Claude reverse-engineer it into a reusable prompt. Paste the image in and ask:

🔎 reverse-engineer a look 
Look at this photo and describe it as a detailed image prompt I can reuse to recreate the same look with my own character. Cover: the pose and crop, the outfit, the background and its colour, the lighting, the mood, and the hair and makeup. Write it as one clean prompt, in plain words, no jargon.

Now you have an exact recipe you can run again, tweak, or hand straight back to Soul with your character. This is how you build a little library of looks that always come out right.

the craft

Get it looking more like you

Feed it 10 to 20 photos The more clean, front-on shots in your folder, the stronger the likeness.
Keep your styling consistent Match your usual hair and look, that is what your reference photos train.
Go high resolution Generate at the highest quality for more face detail and fewer artefacts.
Change one thing at a time Swap just the background or the outfit so you learn what actually works.
