the outcome

What you're doing

You take one talking-head clip and restyle the entire thing by typing. Omni Flash does the cuts, the motion graphics, even the text pop-ups. Swap the background, change the palette, add captions, all in plain English. You can run three totally different looks off the same clip.

step 1

Connect Arcads to Claude

  1. 🧩 In Claude, go to Customize, then Connectors
  2. 🔎 Type in Arcads and click connect
step 2

Paste a style prompt with your video

Upload your clip, paste one of the style prompts below, and ask Claude to use Google's Omni Flash. That's it, it restyles the whole clip. Each prompt keeps your face, your voice, and your mic exactly as filmed, and only changes the look.

🎨 style 1 · paper collage / stop-motion 
Edit this uploaded video and keep my face, body proportions, skin texture, clothing, movement, timing, gestures, camera framing, original voice, and original audio exactly the same. Keep me photorealistic and unchanged. Do not turn me into a cartoon, a drawing, or an animated character. Preserve the microphone (black desk mic with a green button and stand) as the main foreground subject, position clearly visible throughout, do not remove, hide, replace, or change it. Restyle the whole clip as a bold mixed-media collage with a stop-motion feel: cut me and the microphone out with a rough paper-cutout edge and a subtle white outline; replace the background with layered crumpled paper, torn newspaper, halftone dots, coloured tape and bold flat paper shapes; use a strong palette of red, acid yellow, black, off-white and small blue accents; add energetic hand-drawn arrows, circles, scribbles and sticker-style elements around me. Add EXACTLY these three text pop-ups, once each, in order, and no other text at all: 1) "EDITED BY AI" 2) "100% AI" 3) "GOOGLE OMNI". Do not transcribe speech or add captions. Slightly choppy stop-motion energy, subtle camera shake, light glitch, but keep my face and the mic clear. Bold, playful, striking.
🎨 style 2 · sleek modern tech 
Edit this uploaded video and keep my face, body proportions, skin texture, clothing, movement, timing, gestures, camera framing, original voice, and original audio exactly the same. Keep me photorealistic and unchanged, no cartoon, drawing, or animation. Preserve the microphone (black desk mic with a green button and stand) as the foreground subject, same position, visible throughout, do not remove, hide, replace, or alter it. Restyle as a sleek modern tech aesthetic: a clean dark background with soft gradient lighting (deep charcoal into warm amber), subtle glassmorphism panels, thin neon accent lines, a gentle depth-of-field glow, and me cut out with a soft rim light. Add minimal kinetic motion graphics: thin animated underlines, small floating UI cards, smooth directional swipes, sparse, never cluttered. Add EXACTLY these three text pop-ups, once each, in order, and no other text at all: 1) "100% AI" 2) "GOOGLE OMNI" 3) "NO CAPCUT". Do not transcribe speech or add captions. Keep my face and the mic clear. Premium, clean, striking.
🎨 style 3 · cinematic / multi-angle 
Edit this uploaded video and keep my face, body proportions, skin texture, clothing, movement, timing, gestures, original voice, and original audio exactly the same. Keep me photorealistic and unchanged, no cartoon, drawing, or animation. Preserve the microphone (black desk mic, green button, stand) in the foreground, same position, visible throughout, never remove, hide, replace, or alter it. Restyle as a cinematic, film-graded look: a rich teal-and-orange grade, soft cinematic bloom, gentle film grain, moody key lighting. Introduce dynamic angle changes and subtle push-ins and pull-outs on the cuts, as if shot on multiple cameras, while keeping my face centred, sharp, and readable in every angle, and the mic clearly in frame. Add restrained motion graphics: clean lower-third accent bars and smooth light streaks on transitions only. Add EXACTLY these three text pop-ups, once each, in order, and no other text at all: 1) "EDITED BY AI" 2) "GOOGLE OMNI" 3) "ONE PROMPT". Do not transcribe speech or add captions. Never cover my face or hide the mic. Cinematic, bold, striking.
The strict rule in each prompt matters: it tells Omni Flash to keep your face and mic untouched and to add only the three pop-ups, so you get a clean restyle instead of a messy one. Change the pop-up words to whatever fits your video.
