the foundation

Step one: build a master resume

Before you tailor anything, you need raw material. Your master resume is one long private document that holds every job, skill, project, tool, and number from your whole career. You never send it to anyone. It's the source Claude tailors from, so the rule is simple: garbage in, garbage out. If the details aren't captured here, they can't show up on the version you actually submit.

💼 Every role, with numbers Each job, each responsibility, rewritten as a result plus a real number (time saved, money moved, people served).
🛠 Skills, tools, and certs Hard skills, software, soft skills, and certifications, all in their own section so they're easy to pull from.
🧩 The stuff you forget Side projects, freelance, volunteer work, internal tools you built, processes you fixed, people you trained, awards.
🏆 A brag bank Your strongest quantified wins pulled to the top, so tailoring later is copy, trim, and match, not rewrite from scratch.

You build this by letting Claude interview you, one focused batch of questions at a time, using prompt 1 below. It pushes for a result and a number on everything, and it's told never to invent a figure for you.

why it holds up

Use a Claude Project, not a normal chat

Here's the part most people miss. If you run this whole thing in one long chat, the quality quietly falls apart. Every model, Claude included, gets worse as a single conversation grows: it starts losing track of details you gave it earlier. That decay has a name, "context rot", measured across 18 models in a 2025 study (Chroma, 2025). Your master resume is long, so a sprawling chat is the worst place to keep it.

A Claude Project solves this. It's a dedicated workspace with its own knowledge that reloads your full context at the start of every new chat, so it never degrades over time. Set it up once:

  1. 📁 Create a new Project In Claude, make a Project called something like "Job search". Everything below lives inside it.
  2. 📎 Drop your master resume into Project Knowledge Once you've built it with prompt 1, save it as the Project's knowledge. Now every chat in this Project already knows your full history.
  3. 📝 Write the instructions once Add a short custom instruction like "You have my master resume in this Project's knowledge. Only use what's in it. Never invent or inflate." You set the rules a single time.
  4. 💬 Open a fresh chat per job One new chat for each role you apply to. It reloads clean context every time, so job number twenty is as sharp as job number one.
the real mechanic

What an ATS actually does

An applicant tracking system is a database with keyword search and a workflow on top, not a delete-bot hunting for reasons to reject you. Recruiters store applications in it and search for the people who match. Your job is not to trick it, it's to make yourself genuinely easy to find and easy to say yes to. Four things move the needle:

🎯 Match the exact job title A resume carrying the posting's real job title lands a 10.6x higher interview rate (Jobscan). Use it wherever it's honestly true of your experience.
🔑 Mirror 15 to 25 real keywords Pull the actual hard skills and tools from the job description and use their exact wording, each a small number of times. No stuffing.
📊 Lead with quantified impact Every bullet opens with a result and a real number. It parses cleanly and it reads well to the human who opens it next.
🧾 Clean single-column, save as .docx Standard headings, contact info in the body text, no tables or text boxes that scramble the parse. A .docx file is the safest format.
🚫 The honesty guardrail. Ignore any tool that promises a "guaranteed ATS score" or tells you to hide keywords in white text. Fake scores are theatre, and fabricated metrics fall apart in the interview when someone asks you to explain the number. Never invent an achievement or a figure. Everything below is built to keep you truthful.

The 3 prompts

Each prompt hires Claude as a different expert. Prompt 1 is your Career Archaeologist, digging every buried win out of your memory. Prompt 3 is the Brutally Honest Recruiter, telling you where you actually stand before you waste an application. Prompt 2 is the ATS keyword-matcher and hiring manager, tailoring a clean resume to one specific posting. A smart order: build once with prompt 1, then for each job run prompt 3 to see the gap, then prompt 2 to write the tailored version.

🗂 prompt 1, the master resume builder, paste into a Claude Project 
You are a sharp career coach and professional resume writer. Your job is to interview me and pull out EVERY accomplishment, skill, and detail worth capturing, then organize it into a "master resume": a long internal document I will never send to anyone, only feed to you later. Rules: Interview me one focused batch of questions at a time, do not dump 30 questions at once, go role by role starting with my most recent job. For every responsibility I give you, push for the RESULT and a NUMBER, use the pattern "Accomplished [X result], measured by [Y number], by doing [Z action]", if I do not have a number ask what changed, who benefited, or how much time or money was involved, and help me estimate honestly, never invent a number for me. Dig for things people forget: side projects, freelance, volunteer work, internal tools I built, processes I improved, people I trained, awards, and praise from performance reviews. Capture hard skills, tools, soft skills, and certifications in their own section. Keep everything truthful, flag anything that sounds inflated and ask me to confirm it really happened. Output when we finish: a clean, clearly sectioned master resume (Experience with quantified bullets, Skills, Projects, Education, Certs, plus a Brag Bank of my strongest quantified wins). Start by asking me for my most recent role and the first set of questions.
✂️ prompt 2, the resume tailor, paste a job description after it 
You have my master resume in this Project's knowledge. I am going to paste a job description. Produce a tailored, one-to-two-page resume for THIS role. Do this in order: 1. Extract from the job description the exact job title, the top 10 to 15 hard skills/tools/keywords, and the 3 to 5 things this role cares about most. 2. Pull ONLY from my master resume the experience and wins that match, do not invent, inflate, or add anything I did not tell you, if the role wants something I lack tell me plainly instead of faking it. 3. Write the resume: use a job title that matches the posting where it is truthful to my experience, mirror the job description's exact wording for hard skills and tools, aim for roughly 15 to 25 relevant keywords each used 1 to 3 times, no keyword stuffing, lead every bullet with a quantified result using my real numbers, single column with standard headings and contact info in the body, no tables or text boxes so it parses cleanly, keep my real voice with no generic filler. 4. After the resume give me a short match report: which key requirements I clearly hit, which are weak, and 2 or 3 honest suggestions. Do NOT give me a fake ATS score or a guaranteed-to-pass claim, qualitative feedback only. Here is the job description: [PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION]
🕵️ prompt 3, the brutally honest recruiter, paste a job description after it 
Act as a brutally honest recruiter who screens for this exact role. You have my master resume in this Project. I will paste a job description. Do NOT rewrite my resume. Diagnose the gap between me and the job. Give me: 1. FIT VERDICT: strong / stretch / long shot, in one line, and why. 2. THE 6-SECOND SKIM: what a recruiter sees in the first 6 seconds and whether it makes them keep reading. 3. MISSING KEYWORDS: hard skills, tools, and the job title from the posting that are absent or weak, ranked by how much they matter. 4. RED FLAGS: gaps, vague claims, or anything that would make you hesitate, plus the interview questions you would ask to test my weak spots. 5. HONEST MOVES: the 3 highest-impact things I could truthfully add or emphasize, if a gap is real and I cannot close it honestly say so, never suggest I fabricate anything. Here is the job description: [PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION]
what to remember

A few honest notes

✅ Truthful always beats optimized. Never let Claude invent a number or an achievement, and don't let yourself either. A real, specific, slightly smaller win reads stronger than an inflated one, and it survives the interview. Every prompt here is built to push you toward accuracy, not away from it.
📄 Clean formatting parses best. Single column, standard section headings, contact details in the body text, no tables, columns, or text boxes, and save as .docx unless the posting asks for PDF. Fancy templates are exactly what confuses the parser.
🌱 This makes you faster and sharper, not guaranteed. It gets you past "generic resume in a pile of hundreds" and helps you apply to the right roles well. It does not manufacture experience you don't have, and no system can promise you the interview. What it buys you is a tailored, honest resume in minutes instead of an afternoon.
