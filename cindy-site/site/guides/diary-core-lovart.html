the look

The vibe we're going for

Think Korean and Japanese Pinterest diary-core: a candid photo that looks like it was printed into a scrapbook and decorated by hand with gel pens. White stars, pastel hearts, a mini polaroid of the same photo, a tiny music player sticker, and handwritten phrases like "one fine day" and "you're sparkling." Cute and a little chaotic, but still tasteful, and your face stays the clear focus.

the setup

The full prompt

Copy this exactly:

🎀 the diary-core prompt 
Transform the uploaded photo into a cute scrapbook-style Instagram post with a candid Y2K flash camera aesthetic.

The image should look like a personal digital camera photo that has been decorated by hand after being printed into a diary or scrapbook. Use soft flash lighting, gentle grain, slightly lifted brightness, natural skin texture, and a warm-cool balanced filter. The filter should feel aesthetic and cohesive even if the original photo has no filter.

Add playful hand-drawn overlays around the subject: imperfect white stars, pastel hearts, sparkles, bows, swirls, tiny clouds, arrows, handwritten words, little symbols, and doodled sticker elements. The doodles should look like they were drawn with gel pens or paint markers directly on the image. Use mostly white with accents of pastel pink, baby blue, pale yellow, mint green, and small touches of red.

Include short handwritten phrases in different sizes, placed naturally around the photo:
"one fine day"
"you're sparkling"
"pretty"
"cute"
"today's song"
"little diary"
"love love love"
"smile"
"look here"

Add one or two collage details, such as a mini Polaroid frame, a small music-player sticker, a tiny heart sticker, or a paper-cutout style caption. Keep these subtle and integrated into the image.

The final result should feel like Korean/Japanese Pinterest diary-core, artsy teen magazine collage, cute chaotic but still tasteful. The subject should remain the clear focus. The face should stay visible and flattering. Preserve the person's real identity and facial features.

Make it visually rich, charming, slightly messy, handmade, nostalgic, and Instagram-worthy.
the craft

Make it yours

The prompt works beautifully as-is, but small tweaks change the whole mood:

✍️ Swap the handwritten phrases. Replace them with your own words, an inside joke, song lyrics, or your name.
🌈 Change the palette. Trade the pastels for "warm browns and cream" (cosy autumn diary) or "black, white and silver" (grunge scrapbook).
📸 Pick your photo wisely. Candid, slightly imperfect shots work better than posed ones. Travel photos with a landmark or skyline behind you are perfect.
🎶 Lean into the collage. Ask for "a ticket stub, a pressed flower sticker and washi tape strips" instead of the music player.
fix it live

Fixing things after it generates

This is what makes Lovart different from a normal image generator: everything stays editable, so you don't have to regenerate when one thing is off.

  • 👆 Want to move something? Use the move object feature. Select a doodle or sticker and drag it wherever you want.
  • ✏️ Change any generated text with the Text Edit feature. Click the words on the image and rewrite them, no regenerating.
  • 📐 Change the angle of your picture with the multi-angle feature. Get a different perspective on the same shot.
