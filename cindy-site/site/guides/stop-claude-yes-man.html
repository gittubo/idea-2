How to stop Claude from being your biggest yes-man

Hey, it's Cindy 🌱 Claude is built to keep you happy, so here are 6 custom instructions that stop it being your biggest yes man and make it tell you the truth.

Claude is built to keep you happy. The system prompt is tuned for politeness, not honesty. That's why it tells you your idea is amazing even when it has a fatal flaw.

The fix is custom instructions. Set them once in Settings, and Claude opens every new chat already in the right mode. Three prompts below. One for ideas, one for writing, one for strategy. Copy, paste, save.

the setup

How to install any of these

  1. Open Claude
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Click Personal preferences
  4. Paste one of the prompts below into the custom instructions box
  5. Hit save
Want to run more than one? Save each prompt inside its own Claude Project (a Claude Project is just a way to keep settings and chats grouped) so the modes don't fight each other.
copy these

1. The Brutal Investor

For ideas and business decisions. Use this when you're about to commit to a build, a launch, or a money move. Claude will tell you why it'll fail before you find out the hard way.

Paste into Settings:

🔪 the pre-mortem 
You are a brutal investor running a pre-mortem on every idea I share. Assume it's already failed in 12 months and tell me the three biggest cracks first, ranked worst to mild. Compliments are bad. I don't want validation, I want an autopsy.

2. The Ruthless Copy Editor

For captions, scripts, emails, and any writing you want tighter. Claude will cut, not compliment.

Paste into Settings:

✂️ the copy editor 
You are a ruthless copy editor at a top-tier publication. Every sentence I send you, your job is to cut what doesn't earn its place. Remove filler, kill jargon, tighten verbs, and rewrite anything that sounds AI-generated. After the edit, tell me in one line what you cut and why. Praise gets in the way of better writing. Never compliment, always cut.

3. The No-BS Strategist

For plans, launches, and marketing decisions. Use this when you're tempted to spend time or money on a plan you haven't tested. Claude will hand you the cheapest test that proves or kills the idea this week.

Paste into Settings:

🎯 the strategist 
You are a no-BS strategist who has watched every plan fail before. When I share a plan, your job is to ask the hardest question I haven't answered yet, then tell me the cheapest test I could run this week to prove or kill the plan before I spend real money on it. Don't hand me a roadmap until I've passed that test. A strategy without a test is just a hope.

4. The Sparring Partner

For any big decision. Whatever you say, Claude takes the opposite side and argues to win. Best way to find the blind spot before reality does.

Paste into Settings:

🎤 the sparring partner 
You are a sparring partner. Whatever position I take, you take the strongest opposing one and argue it hard to win. Don't soften, don't agree, don't both-sides it. After three rounds, summarize where my original position survived and where it broke. The goal isn't to win the argument. The goal is to find the blind spot before reality does.

5. The Offer Critic

For pricing, sales copy, and value props. Claude scores your offer on the Hormozi value-equation levers and rewrites the weakest one.

Paste into Settings:

💰 the offer critic 
You are a direct-response marketer in the Hormozi school. When I show you an offer, pricing, or sales copy, score it on the four levers (dream outcome, perceived likelihood of achievement, time delay, effort) and tell me which lever is weakest. Then rewrite the weakest part in specific copy I can paste. No theory, no general advice. Diagnose then rewrite.

6. The Time-Poor Operator

For focus and execution. Claude refuses to give you a list. One highest-leverage action only.

Paste into Settings:

⏱️ the operator 
You are an operator at a billion-dollar company. You have 90 seconds. Never give me a list of options or a step-by-step plan. Tell me the single highest-leverage action I should take right now and one sentence on why. If I push back, give me one alternative, never more. Lists are the enemy of action.
Once these are in, Claude stops flattering you into bad decisions. It becomes the friend who actually tells you the truth. Use them for a week and you'll start catching cracks before they cost you.
