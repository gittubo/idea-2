The 5 study modes, in order

Type the word, then your topic. Here's what each mode gives you, then the fuller prompt that makes it actually good.

  1. 📝 Study notes. Turns any chapter into the short, important points, so you study the essentials instead of re-reading everything.
  2. 🎨 Visualize. Turns the topic into a diagram, flow, or table so the structure clicks instantly.
  3. ✍️ Handwritten. Explains the topic so simply a 12-year-old would get it, perfect for the stuff that just won't go in.
  4. 🃏 Flashcards. Turns the topic into question-and-answer cards for fast, active recall.
  5. 🎯 Exam mode. Quizzes you, marks your answers, and tells you exactly where you're weak.

The 5 prompts, ready to paste

📝 study notes 
Act as my study coach. Turn the topic below into clear study notes: the key points only, grouped under short headings, with a one-line "why it matters" on each. Skip the filler and bold the terms I'd actually get tested on.

Topic: [paste your chapter or topic]
🎨 visualize 
Turn the topic below into a simple visual I can study from: a labelled diagram, flowchart, or table that shows how the parts connect. Use plain labels, keep it clean, and add a one-sentence caption explaining what the visual shows.

Topic: [paste your topic]
✍️ handwritten (explain like I'm 12) 
Explain the topic below like you're teaching a curious 12-year-old: short sentences, everyday examples, and no jargon unless you define it in the same breath. Then give me one analogy I'll actually remember.

Topic: [paste the thing you keep not getting]
🃏 flashcards 
Make me flashcards from the topic below. Give me 15 question-and-answer pairs, question on one line, answer on the next, ordered easy to hard. Focus on what I'm most likely to be tested on, and keep each answer to one or two sentences.

Topic: [paste your topic]
🎯 exam mode 
Put me in exam mode on the topic below. Ask me one question at a time, wait for my answer, then mark it, correct me, and tell me what to review before the next question. Mix recall questions with "explain why" questions.

After 10, give me a score and my three weakest areas. Topic: [paste your topic]

6 more teacher-mode words to try

"Summarize in 5 bullets" The fastest way to preview a dense topic before you dive into the detail.
"Teach it backwards" Start from the answer or outcome and work back to the basics. Great for problem-solving subjects.
"Common mistakes" Ask for the top errors people make on this topic so you avoid them on the test.
"Real-world example" Turns an abstract concept concrete with a story you'll actually remember.
"Feynman it" Ask it to explain the topic, then explain it back and have it grade your explanation for gaps.
"Memory hooks" Ask for mnemonics or acronyms for the lists and sequences you have to memorise.
Here's the real trick: chain them. Notes, then visualize, then flashcards, then exam mode on the same topic, and you've done a full study cycle in about 20 minutes. The word just tells ChatGPT which mode to switch into. Save this and run it on your next hard topic.
