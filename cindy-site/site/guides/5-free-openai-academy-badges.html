The order, and why it's this order

Each one assumes the one before it. Foundations teaches you to talk to the model, Applied turns that into a repeatable process, Agents hands that process to something that runs it for you, Leadership is about running it across other people, and Scope is the builder's one.

If you only do two, do the first two. That's about two and a half hours and it's the pair that changes how you work day to day.

  1. 1. AI Foundations · about 70 minutes The basics of AI, large language models and ChatGPT. You practise giving clear instructions, adding context, reviewing outputs and using AI responsibly. Five parts: Introduction, Large Language Models, Prompting and Evaluation, Add Context, Responsible Use.
    Go to the course →
  2. 2. Applied AI Foundations · about 80 minutes You take one recurring task from your own job and turn it into a repeatable workflow: break the work into steps, find where ChatGPT helps, and build review points in. Five parts: From Prompts to Workflows, Reasoning Models, Choose Capabilities, Design the Workflow, Responsible Use in Workflows.
    Go to the course →
  3. 3. Agents and Workflows · about 90 minutes This is the one that changes how you hand work over. Its own first module is called "From Asking to Assigning Work". You give an agent context, define the output, set boundaries, review drafts and reuse what works.
    Go to the course →
  4. 4. AI Leadership · 3 hours The long one, and it's about running AI projects rather than doing the work yourself. Take it if you lead a team or you're trying to get AI adopted somewhere that's resisting it.
    Go to the course →
  5. 5. Scope AI Solutions · 30 minutes The shortest, and the only one written for people who already code. It sits under OpenAI's "For builders" path, not the knowledge-worker one, so do it last or skip it if you don't build.

How the badge works. Every course ends in an assessment. Score 80 percent or higher and the badge is issued through Accredible and emailed to you. You can retake it.

Three things worth knowing before you start

They're badges, not certifications OpenAI says so itself: Academy badges and pathway certificates of completion "are not certifications". They're still real proof you did the training, just don't write "certified by OpenAI" on your CV.
Nothing here needs a paid plan Every course is free on any ChatGPT account, including Free. You only sign in so your progress saves.
There are 14 courses, not 5 Across four paths. These five are the ones worth your first eight hours. AI for Educators and AI for College Students are 45 minutes each if either fits you.
Do them on a real task Applied AI Foundations only works if you bring an actual recurring task from your own job. Pick it before you start, not during.
