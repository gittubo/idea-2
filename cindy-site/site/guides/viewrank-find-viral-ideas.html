the mindset

Why outlier score beats raw views

Raw views lie. A reel with 2M views from an account with 5M followers is a quiet underperformer. A reel with 180k views from a 12k-follower account is a rocket, the algorithm pushed it way past the creator's own audience. That second one is the idea worth studying, and sorting by views alone hides it.

An outlier score fixes this. It divides a video's views by that account's own baseline (its median recent views), so it measures how far a post broke out relative to what's normal for them. As a rough read: around 10x the baseline is a strong outlier, and 100x is a monster. Score, not view count, is what tells you an idea has real pull independent of follower size.

🎯 Where to point it. Run this on 5 to 10 creators in your exact niche, plus 2 or 3 slightly adjacent ones. Adjacent niches are where fresh formats leak in before everyone in your lane copies them.
the setup

Set up ViewRank in about 4 minutes

  1. 🧩 Install the extension. Open the ViewRank Chrome Web Store page and click Add to Chrome. It's free, and it processes the profile data locally in your browser unless you choose to send videos to its paid workspace.
  2. 📌 Pin it to your toolbar. Click the puzzle-piece Extensions icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to ViewRank. Now it's one click from any page instead of buried in a menu.
  3. 🎬 Open a creator's Reels tab. Go to any public Instagram profile and click the Reels tab specifically (the URL ends in /reels/). ViewRank reads the grid on that tab, so start it there, not on the main profile.
  4. 🖱️ Click the ViewRank icon. Pick how many reels to load (50 to 500, or All) and let it scroll the profile to pull the numbers. Bigger accounts take longer, so start with 200 to keep it quick.
  5. 📊 Sort by outlier score. In the panel, switch the sort to Outliers. The account's biggest breakout reels jump to the top. From there you can also star favourites, add tags and notes, and export the list to a sheet.
💡 Same trick works on TikTok. ViewRank now supports TikTok profiles too, so you can pull outliers from a creator's TikTok grid the same way and spot formats before they cross over to Reels.
what to look for

Read the board like a strategist

Once the outliers are sorted, don't just watch them. Reverse-engineer them. For each top reel, break down these four layers, because the score tells you it worked but not why.

🪝 The hook The first 2 seconds. What is the exact line and visual that stops the scroll? Write it down word for word. Most of the outlier is decided here.
🧱 The structure How is the middle built? Is it a list, a story, a single demo, a myth-then-truth? Map the beats in order so you can reuse the skeleton.
🎯 The angle What tension or promise is it playing on? The same topic with a sharper angle is often the whole reason one reel beat another on the same profile.
📦 The packaging The on-screen cover text, the caption, the CTA. This is the free half of the reel most people skip. Screenshot the ones that made you tap.
🎯 Look for patterns, not one-offs. If a creator has three outliers and all three open with a bold claim then immediately show proof, that's a repeatable format for your niche, not a fluke. Formats that repeat are the ones worth stealing the structure of.

make it yours

Model it, never copy it

This is the line that keeps you original and keeps you out of trouble. You take the skeleton Claude pulled out and rebuild it with your topic, your examples, and your voice. Same proven structure, completely different content.

rebuild it in your voice 
Here's a proven reel skeleton:
[paste the skeleton Claude gave you]

My niche: [e.g. AI tools for non-technical marketers]
My voice: warm, plain-spoken, no hype, I show the actual build
My topic for this one: [your specific topic]

Write me 3 different reel scripts that use this exact structure
but with my topic and voice. For each: give the hook line, the
beats, and one line of on-screen cover text. Do not reuse the
original creator's words, examples, or phrasing anywhere.
🚫 Copying the words is where it goes wrong. Reusing someone's hook line, script, or examples verbatim reads as a knock-off and can get your reel suppressed or your account reported. Take the format, bring your own substance. The structure is fair game, the content has to be yours.
other free tools

ViewRank alternatives worth knowing

ViewRank is my default, but these do the same job with different strengths. All have free versions, so try a couple and keep the one that fits how you browse.

🔎 Outliers Instagram only, free. Filters a Reels tab down to just the posts doing 5x or more views relative to the account's followers, with a side panel, saved library, and CSV export. Great when you only want the true breakouts and nothing else. Get it
🏷️ Handler Free, and it works differently: it overlays an outlier score on every post as you normally browse Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube, no separate panel to open. Best if you'd rather see scores live while scrolling than sort a whole profile at once. Get it
📈 ClipRanker Free version available. Analyses any public profile across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube, sorts by views, likes, comments, or engagement rate, filters by content type, and flags outliers against the creator's last 20 posts. Strong if you work across all three platforms. Get it
⭐ Why I stay on ViewRank It covers Instagram and TikTok, sorts by true outlier score, and lets me star, tag, note, and export the winners into a swipe file. That last part is what turns a browsing session into an actual idea bank.
