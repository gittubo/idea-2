Free vs paid AI tools: 6 swaps that do the same job for $0

Hey, it's Cindy 🌱 Half my DMs are people asking if a paid AI tool is "worth it," when there's usually a free twin doing the same job. Here are 6 swaps I actually trust.

swap one

1. Make images

Paid

Midjourney (from about $10/mo, no free plan).

Free

Nano Banana ($0), Google's Gemini image model, great for product shots, edits, and consistent characters. Free in the Gemini app with a generous daily limit (not unlimited).

2. Write copy

Paid

Jasper AI (from about $69/mo).

Free

Claude ($0), its free tier writes captions, emails, and ad angles that sound human and need the least cleanup. It's my daily writing driver. The only limit is a message cap.

swap three

3. Generate video

Paid

Runway (from about $15/mo).

Free

Dreamina (by ByteDance/CapCut, $0), 225 free credits every day that reset every 24 hours, no card. Honest catch: free renders are watermarked and lower resolution, and CapCut's terms describe the service as private, non-commercial use, so it's for drafts and personal projects, not client delivery.

4. Make avatar videos

Paid

HeyGen (from about $29/mo).

Free

Hedra ($0), turns a photo into a talking-head avatar. Honest catch: the free tier covers a few short clips, not ongoing production.

swap five

5. Design and decks

Paid

Canva Pro (from about $18/mo).

Free

Lovart ($0), an AI design agent for logos, posters, and brand kits from a prompt. Free plan is about 100 credits a day (roughly 5 to 10 images), personal use.

6. Schedule posts

Paid

Buffer (from about $6/mo per channel).

Free

Publer ($0), its free plan queues and auto-posts to up to 3 accounts. Note: X/Twitter isn't included on the free plan.

the craft

How to use this

You don't have to switch everything. Try the free twin for a job you currently pay for, and if it does what you need, keep the money. I still pay for a couple I genuinely love, so use the free version to decide whether the paid one earns its keep.
