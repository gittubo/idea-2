build it this week

The context files, adapted

The single idea worth taking from video one. Open Claude, start a project, and paste this. It interviews you, then writes the files.

🗂 build my marketing context files 
I want you to build the context files that everything else I ask you about marketing will read from. Interview me first, then write them.

Ask me no more than eight questions in total, one at a time, covering: what the business sells and to whom, what a good month looks like in numbers, who the ideal customer is and what they are actually trying to solve, who we lose deals to and why, what we say about ourselves and what customers say back, and which channels we actually use today.

Then write me five short documents: goals, ideal customer, competitors, positioning, and channels. Keep each one under 400 words, write in plain sentences rather than bullet fragments, and mark anything you inferred rather than heard from me so I can correct it.

Finally, tell me which single file is weakest based on what I gave you, and what I would need to find out to fix it.
Then reuse them. Every time you ask for a campaign, a caption, a landing page or an email, attach these files first. That is the whole mechanism, and it is why the output stops sounding generic.

Check whether an assistant would cite you

The practical version of video four, runnable in ten minutes on a page you already have.

🔎 the AI citation check 
Here is a page from my website: [paste the URL and the full page text].

Act as an AI assistant deciding whether to cite this page when someone asks about [the topic this page covers]. Work through it properly rather than giving me a score.

First, write the five questions a real person would actually type or say about this topic, phrased the way people talk rather than the way keywords look.

Then, for each question, tell me whether this page answers it directly, partly, or not at all, and quote the exact sentence you would have cited if you were citing it. If there is no such sentence, say so plainly.

Finally, give me a rewrite plan: the specific lines to add, where to put them, and what to change about the page title, the first paragraph and the headings so the answer is findable in the first screen rather than buried halfway down.
