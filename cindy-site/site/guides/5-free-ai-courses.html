match to your goal

Pick by what you actually want

how to actually finish

Don't collect courses, finish one

The reason people have ten half-done courses is that they treat starting as the win. Flip it: one finished course with something to show beats five you bookmarked.

Pick one, tied to a goal Choose the single course that maps to what you actually want next, not the most impressive-sounding one.
Block the time Put two short sessions a week in your calendar. A course with no time booked never gets finished.
Build as you learn Apply each module to a tiny real project, so the learning sticks and you end with a portfolio piece.
Add the cert When you finish, put the certificate and the project on your LinkedIn the same day. Proof beats a claim.

Let Claude turn the course into a plan you will actually complete:

🗺️ turn a course into a plan + a project 
I am about to start [course name] and I want to actually finish it and have something to show, not just collect another certificate. Build me a realistic weekly study plan based on how the course is structured, assuming I have about [X hours] a week, and for each module tell me the one key idea to take away.

Then design a small, real project I can build alongside the course using what I learn, so by the end I have a portfolio piece and a certificate, not just a certificate. Tell me what to do in week one so I start today.
