Travel with Claude: your stress-free trip setup guide

Hey, it's Cindy 🌱 Below is the exact setup I use to plan and run every trip with Claude. About 10 minutes to set up, works for any destination.

before you start

What you'll need before you start

  • A Claude account, free tier works, but Claude Pro or Max unlocks Projects, Cowork (scheduled tasks), and computer use. (claude.ai)
  • Your trip details in one place: flights, hotels, rough itinerary, dietary needs, total budget
  • About 10 minutes
the setup

Step 1: Set up a Claude Project for your trip

Think of a Claude Project as a dedicated folder where Claude remembers everything about your trip. You paste your info in once, and every conversation inside that Project automatically knows your flights, hotels, allergies, budget, no re-explaining.

Quick setup

  1. Open claude.ai → click Projects in the sidebar → New Project
  2. Name it something obvious like Japan Trip Oct 2026
  3. In the Project instructions / knowledge box, paste:
    • Your flights (dates, times, flight numbers, booking refs)
    • Your hotels (names, addresses, check-in/out dates, confirmation codes)
    • Your rough itinerary (city by city, day by day, bullet points are fine)
    • Your dietary needs (allergies, vegan, halal, coeliac, etc.)
    • Your total budget + home currency (e.g. "AUD $4,000 total")
    • Your interests (e.g. "love ramen, hate crowds, want one nice splurge dinner")
  4. Hit save. Start chatting.
💡 Why this works: Claude loads everything in the Project into every conversation. It's like handing your entire trip binder to a concierge who never forgets a detail.
🔒 Privacy note: Your data is encrypted and Anthropic doesn't sell it. But you don't even need to share sensitive info, booking reference codes are enough for Claude to plan your day. Skip the credit card numbers and passport scans.
every morning

Automation 1: Daily morning briefing

Every morning of your trip, open your Claude Project and type:

"brief me on today"

Claude pulls together:

  • Today's weather for your location
  • Your scheduled activities in order
  • Travel times between each stop
  • Time-sensitive details, free museum entry before 10am, hotel check-out deadlines, restaurant reservation times, last train home

Your entire day, summarised in 30 seconds.

Pro level: make it fully automatic

Instead of asking Claude every morning, set up a Claude Cowork scheduled task so the briefing lands in your inbox every morning at 7am while you're still in bed.

How:

  1. In your Project chat, type: "every morning at 7am, send me a daily briefing for that day of my trip"
  2. Claude will offer to create a scheduled task, confirm it
  3. Done. You'll get a daily push every morning of the trip.
the magic one

Automation 2: Auto-book reservations

This is the magic one. With Claude's computer use (Pro/Max), Claude can open your browser, navigate to a restaurant's booking page, and fill in the reservation form for you, even on Japanese, Korean or French sites.

You review it, hit confirm, done. No copy-pasting, no Google Translate, no phrasebook.

How to use it

  1. Inside your Project, paste the restaurant link
  2. Type: "book a table for 2 on [date] at [time], my name is [name], phone [number]"
  3. Claude opens the browser, fills in the form, asks you to confirm before submitting
📌 Tip: computer use is in research preview, give it a simple single-page booking first to get a feel. It handles things like Tabelog, OpenTable, and most restaurant sites beautifully.
eating safely

Automation 3: Your personal allergen detector

Tell Claude your dietary needs once in the Project setup, coeliac, nut allergy, halal, vegan, dairy-free, whatever, and it remembers.

From then on, every time you send Claude a restaurant link or a menu photo, it will:

  • Scan the menu
  • Flag what's safe, what's risky, and what to ask the waiter about
  • No more guessing in a language you don't speak

Pro tip: the "dietary card"

Ask Claude:

"write me a dietary card in [local language] explaining my allergies that I can show waiters on my phone."

You'll get a polite, properly translated paragraph that explains your allergies in the local language. Screenshot it. Show it to staff. Done.

💬 A dietary card has saved more travellers than travel insurance.
the money

Automation 4: Budget tracking in plain English

Forget spreadsheets.

Throughout your trip, just message Claude as you spend:

"¥3,200 on lunch, ¥850 on the train, ¥12,000 on a hotel night"

Claude logs each expense to the Project. Then anytime you want a check-in, ask:

"how's my budget doing?"

You'll get a live summary by category, converted to your home currency, with how many days you have left and what you've got remaining per day.

At the end of the trip, ask for a full breakdown by category: flights, food, transport, activities, shopping. Budgeting without the admin.

Setup

  • In the Project instructions: add your total budget + home currency (e.g. "$4,000 AUD total budget")
  • That's it. Just start logging spends as you go.
what to pack

Automation 5: Weather-smart packing list

A few days before you fly, ask Claude:

"build me a packing list for this trip"

Claude cross-references the 14-day forecast with your itinerary: hiking day vs. temple visit vs. fancy dinner vs. city walking, and builds a packing list that actually matches what you'll do.

It also flags cultural dress codes like covered shoulders for temples, or smart casual for nicer restaurants.

Bonus: let Claude style you

Take photos of your wardrobe and upload them to the Project. Then ask: "using only the clothes I own, build me 7 outfits for this trip." Claude will suggest combinations based on weather + activity + dress code.

Mid-trip use

Walking out the door at night and not sure if it'll be cold? Ask:

"do I need a jacket tonight?"

Claude already knows where you're going and the forecast. Instant answer.

when plans break

Bonus: instant replan when things change

This is the one that saves trips.

Flight delayed? Raining on your outdoor day? Restaurant closed unexpectedly?

Just message your Claude Project:

"it's raining, our hike is cancelled, what should we do instead?"

Claude already knows:

  • Your full itinerary
  • What you've already done
  • Opening hours of nearby alternatives
  • Travel times from where you are now

It reshuffles your entire day in seconds and suggests alternatives that fit your schedule. No more panicked Googling on a 4G signal in the rain.

do this now

The 10-minute checklist

Before your next trip, do these in order:

  1. Open Claude → Projects → New Project, name it after your trip
  2. Paste flights, hotels, itinerary, dietary needs, budget into Project instructions
  3. Add your interests + home currency
  4. Set up the morning briefing scheduled task (Cowork)
  5. Generate your dietary card in the local language and screenshot it
  6. Generate your packing list 3-5 days before flying
  7. Use the Project as your travel concierge for the entire trip

That's it. Reuse the same setup for every future trip, just create a new Project each time.
