the honest four

The 4 certificates

  1. 🟦 Agentblazer Champion 2026 (Salesforce) Free badge, about 10 hours. The best free hands-on signal you can earn right now: you actually build an AI agent in Salesforce's playground rather than watching slides. Honest label: it's a badge with yearly renewal, not a formal certification, and it says "I do the work" rather than "I passed an exam". Best for: beginners who want a brand-name, hands-on start for zero dollars. trailhead.salesforce.com/agentblazer
  2. 🟨 Generative AI Leader (Google Cloud) $99 proctored exam, 10 to 15 hours of prep, no prerequisites. The accessible "real exam" on this list: 90 minutes, proctored, and designed for people who lead or work with AI rather than build it. Best for: managers, marketers, and founders who want one credible, examined AI line on their profile. cloud.google.com (Gen AI Leader)
  3. 🟩 Certified Associate: Generative AI and LLMs (NVIDIA) $125 proctored exam, 10 to 20 hours of prep. One of the strongest logos in AI on your credentials list, remotely proctored, valid two years. Honest read: it signals literacy rather than deep capability, but it's cheap, real, and the brand carries. Best for: career changers who want a credible proctored credential without an engineering background. nvidia.com (NCA-GENL)
  4. 🟥 AI Governance Professional, AIGP (IAPP) $649 to $799 exam, 40 to 60 hours over 6 to 8 weeks. The heavyweight, and the governance cert AI risk roles ask for by name. AI governance, risk, and compliance roles are exploding because every company deploying AI suddenly needs someone accountable for it. Best for: anyone in legal, risk, compliance, HR, or operations who wants to own the AI-responsibility lane. iapp.org/certify/aigp
🚫 Retired certs still being recommended elsewhere. Microsoft's Azure AI Engineer AI-102 was retired on 30 June 2026 (replaced by the AI-103), and Salesforce's Certified AI Associate was retired in February 2026. If a list includes either, the list is stale. Certificates churn fast now: always check the issuer's page before you enrol.

Which one is worth it for YOU?

$0 and hands-on Agentblazer. Build the agent, earn the badge, and you'll know within ten hours whether the agent world excites you.
Non-technical, need credibility Google's Gen AI Leader. A proctored exam without prerequisites is the sweet spot for one strong LinkedIn line.
Brand-weight on a budget NVIDIA. $125 for one of the most recognisable logos in AI is the best value-per-dollar here.
Career-defining move AIGP. Expensive and demanding, and the one that opens a dedicated AI governance career lane.
🌱 The rule that beats every certificate. One cert plus one thing you actually built beats five certs every time. Pick from this list, then spend the money you saved on time building something you can demo.
