from the reel

The 5 you already saw

  • anatomy Breaks your product into its parts and adds callout lines with a label on each one.
  • timeline Lays events out left to right with dates and a marker at each point.
  • comparison Puts your product beside the cheap alternative with the differences labelled.
  • infographic Turns a process into one clean graphic with icons, arrows and the text already placed.
  • storyboard Returns your ad idea as panels, so you know which shots to film before you pick up a camera.
the other 150

Product photography

  • flatlay Shoots straight down on the product styled with a few props on a flat surface.
  • macro Extreme close-up on texture, with everything else falling out of focus.
  • knolling Lays every part out separately, parallel, evenly spaced, shot from above.
  • floating Suspends the product in mid air with its ingredients or props frozen around it.
  • cross-section Cuts the product in half so the inside is visible.
  • reflection Stands the product on a mirrored surface so it doubles.
  • cutout Pure white background, no shadow, marketplace ready.
  • backlit Puts the light behind the product so edges glow and anything translucent lights up.
  • golden hour Warm low late-afternoon sun with long soft shadows.
  • hard shadow One hard light source throwing a sharp graphic shadow across the frame.
  • negative space Puts the product small in one corner and leaves most of the frame empty for your headline.
  • monochrome Puts the whole scene in one colour family, product, background and props.

  • offer Sale layout with a price or discount badge as the loudest element.
  • bundle Groups several items together as one set.

Three things that make these work better

The code goes FIRST ChatGPT weights the opening of your prompt most heavily, so the treatment word has to lead. "anatomy, my kettle" beats "my kettle, make it an anatomy diagram".
One code at a time Stacking three codes gives you a muddle of all three. Run them separately and pick, which is also how you end up with a week of posts from one photo.
Feed it a clean photo Flat light, plain background, product filling most of the frame. Every code below inherits the quality of what you upload.
Say what it's for Add "for a carousel slide" or "for a 9:16 story" and the layout changes to suit. The code sets the style, you set the shape.
