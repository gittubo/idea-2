the idea

What Okara actually is

Okara is an AI CMO (chief marketing officer) that runs your marketing on autopilot. Instead of one tool that writes captions and a different tool that checks your SEO, it is a whole team of specialist agents working in the background at the same time.

It is built for business owners, solopreneurs, founders, and marketing specialists who want the output of a marketing department without paying for one. Okara puts the rough replacement value at around $14,000 a month of hires: a marketing lead, an SEO agency, a content writer, a social manager, and a community growth person. One login covers all of it.

the setup

How it works

  1. 🔗 Add your website. Paste in your URL, personal or business, it does not matter which.
  2. 🧠 It scrapes your whole site. Okara reads every page to understand your brand, your offer, and your voice inside out. No long onboarding form to fill in.
  3. 🤖 It spins up your agents. Within minutes you get a dashboard of agents, each with daily and weekly action items already drafted and waiting.
  4. ✅ You approve, it works. Nothing goes live without you. Every agent drafts the work and waits for your yes, which keeps your brand voice yours.
the lineup

The agents doing the work for you

This is the part that makes it feel like a real team. You started with the headline ones in the reel, here is the full lineup.

  1. 🔍 SEO agent: finds your keyword gaps and the technical issues holding your rankings back, then drafts the fixes and full blog posts for approval.
  2. 🌐 GEO agent: gets your brand cited inside AI answers like ChatGPT, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. This is the new SEO, and most people are not doing it yet.
  3. 👽 Reddit agent: finds threads where your brand actually fits and drafts a genuine reply, so you show up in the conversation instead of spamming it.
  4. 🟧 Hacker News agent: spots the right moments to join a discussion and drafts the comment for you to post.
  5. ✖️ X agent: drafts posts and full threads in your voice, ready to edit and ship.
  6. 💼 LinkedIn agent: drafts professional posts and content ideas tied to your brand.
  7. ✍️ Writer agent: handles long-form articles and content, all matched to how you actually sound.
  8. ⚙️ Coding agent: automates the technical SEO fixes on your site, not just flags them.
  9. 🎬 UGC videos agent: guided briefs plus AI video clips you can download for social and ads.
the test

What it found on my own site

I ran it on my own portfolio to test it. Within minutes it told me everything that was off: readability problems, missing metadata, and a list of other gaps I had no idea were there. The best part, it did not just point at the problems, it handed me a list of fixes I could apply right then.

That is the whole pitch in one moment. It is like having a marketing lead audit your site on day one, then keep working every single day after that.

pick your level

How to get the most out of it

🌱 If you are just starting: add your site, then work through the daily action items one at a time. You do not need to touch every agent at once. Pick SEO and GEO first, they compound the longest.

If you want to go deeper: connect Google Search Console and Google Analytics (GA4). Okara then uses your real ranking and traffic data to prioritise, so the suggestions get sharper the more it sees.

For everyone: keep the approval step. The drafts are strong, but a 30 second edit to add your own take is what keeps everything sounding like you and not a robot.
