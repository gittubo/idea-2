the outcome

What you're making

A full photoreal fashion shoot of yourself, editorial covers, street style, campaign shots, generated from a folder of your own photos. No camera, no set. Soul 2.0 locks your real face, so it's you in every frame, in outfits and lighting you never actually shot.

step 1

Connect Higgsfield to Claude

  1. 🧩 In Claude, go to Customize, then Connectors
  2. ➕ Add a custom connector
  3. 🔗 Paste this URL: https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp, then Connect and approve the sign-in
This works on free Claude too. You're allowed one custom connector, and you may need to toggle it on per chat (the + next to the message box, then Connectors).
step 2

Open Claude Code and point it at your photos

So Claude can actually see your pictures, use Claude Code and start a new chat in the folder where your photos live (put 10 to 20 clear photos of yourself in one folder, say a folder called me).

step 3

Make a character of yourself

Ask Claude to build your character with Higgsfield's Soul 2.0, pointed at that folder:

🧬 create your character 
Using the Higgsfield MCP, create a Soul 2.0 character of me from the photos in this folder [point to your folder]. Keep it photoreal and true to my face.
step 4

Grab inspiration on Pinterest

While it builds your character, go to Pinterest and save any shoot you love, editorial, street style, a campaign look, whatever you want to recreate. Screenshot 3 to 6 of them.

step 5

Recreate the shoot as you

Paste the inspiration in and ask Claude to recreate it with your character:

🪄 the shoot 
Recreate these shots, but as me, using my character. Match the poses, lighting and styling. Give me the full set.

Claude generates the whole shoot in one go, straight from your folder. Ask for more outfits or a different vibe and it keeps going.

The links

📸 Higgsfield: higgsfield.ai (the MCP connect URL is https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp)
🤖 Claude: claude.com (connect Higgsfield under Customize, then Connectors)
