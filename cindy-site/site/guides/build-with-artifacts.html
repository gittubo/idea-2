Money and budgeting

Monthly budget tracker

See where the money actually goes 
Build me a simple monthly budget tracker where I type in my income and my expenses grouped by category like rent, food, and fun. Show me what is left over each month and gently flag any category where I go over the amount I planned. Add a small chart so I can see where my money actually goes at a glance, and let me rename or add categories just by telling you.

Savings goal tracker

Watch the goal fill up 
Create a savings goal tracker where I enter a goal name, the total amount I want to save, and how much I have set aside so far. Show me a progress bar that fills up as I add deposits, plus how much is left and an estimate of when I will hit the goal based on my usual monthly amount. Let me keep a few goals at once and mark one as done when I reach it.

Debt payoff planner

Snowball and avalanche, side by side 
Make me a debt payoff planner where I list each debt with its balance, interest rate, and minimum payment. Show me two ways to pay it all off, the snowball method (smallest balance first) and the avalanche method (highest interest first), and tell me roughly how many months each one takes. Let me type in one extra amount I can pay each month and watch the payoff date move earlier.

Bill due-date calendar

Never miss a due date 
Build me a bill calendar where I add each recurring bill with its name, amount, and the day of the month it is due. Lay them out across the month so I can see what is coming up and add a running total of everything due in the current week. Let me tick a bill as paid so it turns grey, and let me mark ones that repeat every month so I do not have to re-enter them.

Subscription audit tool

Find what to cancel 
Create a subscription audit tool where I list every app and service I pay for, the cost, and how often it bills me (monthly or yearly). Add up my true monthly and yearly totals, and let me tag each one as keep, cancel, or unsure so I can see how much I would save by cutting the extras. Sort the list by cost so the biggest drains sit at the top.

Bill splitter

Settle up with the fewest payments 
Build me a bill splitter for group dinners and trips where I type in each person and what they paid or owe. Work out who owes who and the smallest set of payments to settle everything up, and let me add a shared tip or tax that gets divided fairly. Show a clean summary I can read out to the table, and let me clear it and start fresh for the next outing.

Take-home pay estimator

What actually lands in your account 
Make me a simple take-home pay estimator where I enter my hourly rate or salary and the hours or days I work. Let me add rough deductions like tax percentage, super or retirement, and any fixed costs, then show me the estimated amount that actually lands in my account weekly, fortnightly, and monthly. Keep it clearly labelled as an estimate and let me tweak the tax percentage to match my situation.

No-spend challenge tracker

Turn saving into a streak 
Create a no-spend challenge tracker where I pick a number of days and mark each day as a success or a slip. Show me my current streak, my longest streak, and a grid of the whole challenge coloured green for wins and red for slips. Let me jot a quick note on any day (like what tempted me) and cheer me on with a friendly message each time I hit a new streak record.

Net worth snapshot

One number, tracked over time 
Build me a net worth snapshot where I list what I own (savings, car, investments) on one side and what I owe (loans, credit cards) on the other. Subtract one from the other to show my current net worth, and let me save a few dated snapshots so I can see whether the number is going up over time. Add a small bar chart comparing my snapshots so progress feels visible.

50/30/20 budget splitter

Plan versus reality 
Make me a 50/30/20 budget splitter where I type in my monthly take-home pay and it shows the suggested amounts for needs (50 percent), wants (30 percent), and savings (20 percent). Let me enter what I actually spent in each of the three buckets so I can compare the plan against reality and see which bucket is out of line. Let me change the percentages if my own targets are different, and keep the maths updating as I type.
