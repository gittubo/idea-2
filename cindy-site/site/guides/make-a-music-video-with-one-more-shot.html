Make a full music video in minutes with One More Shot

the outcome

What you're making

A complete music video for your own track, generated in one pass and cut to the beat, not stitched together clip by clip. No film crew, no editing timeline. And because you make the song yourself, the music is fully licensed, it's yours.

step 1

Make the song in Suno

Start with the track. Make it in Suno (or use any song you own the rights to). One More Shot makes the video, not the song, so this is where your music comes from.

step 2

Turn it into a video in One More Shot

  1. 🎧 Drop in your song. Upload the track (or paste your Suno link) into One More Shot.
  2. 🎨 Pick your look. Choose a style: cinematic, anime, retro VHS, and more. This sets the whole vibe of the video.
  3. ⚙️ Bump the quality up first. Before you generate, set it to the highest quality in the settings. It makes a huge difference to the output, and you can't add quality back after.
  4. ▶️ Generate. It listens to the arrangement and builds the whole video to match the track, in one run. A few minutes later you've got a full music video.
Not happy with one part? You can regenerate an individual section without redoing the whole video, so you're never stuck with a bad take.
