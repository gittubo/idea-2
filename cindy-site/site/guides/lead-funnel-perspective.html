Build a whole lead funnel from one Claude prompt (Perspective MCP)

Here is where most ad budgets quietly leak: not on the ad itself, but on everything that happens after the click. The landing page, the follow-up emails, the wiring that connects it all to your contact list. Building that for one campaign is slow. Building it again for every new offer is what actually eats your week.

This fixes that. Perspective is a funnel platform, and it now has a real MCP connector you can plug into Claude. Once it is connected, you stop dragging boxes around a builder. You describe the funnel in plain English, and Claude builds the landing page, writes the email sequence, and connects it to your contacts in one chat. You paste the prompt once, swap in your offer, and review a preview before anything goes live.

the four jobs

What this does

It turns one Claude chat into your whole funnel team, working in order so nothing gets skipped.

  1. 🧲 Builds the lead-magnet page. Claude opens Perspective and builds a landing page for your offer, with the hook, the form, and the layout done for you.
  2. ✉️ Writes the follow-up sequence. It drafts a 5-email nurture flow: email 1 delivers the resource, emails 2 to 4 build trust with quick wins, email 5 makes a soft pitch.
  3. 🔗 Connects it to your list. It wires the page to your contact list so new leads flow straight in, no manual export.
  4. 🌐 Publishes to your domain. Once you approve the preview, it pushes the funnel live on your own domain.
one-time setup

How to set up the connector

This is a one-time setup. After this, Claude can drive Perspective for you in any chat.

  1. 🛠 Open the connector settings. In Claude, go to Customize, then Connectors, then Add custom connector.
  2. 🔑 Paste the Perspective MCP URL. Name it Perspective and paste in the Perspective MCP server URL (grab it from your Perspective account settings).
  3. ✅ Sign in and connect. When Claude asks, sign in to your Perspective account to authorize it. That is it, the connector is live.

The mega prompt

Paste this into Claude once the Perspective connector is on, then swap in your own offer.

📋 the whole funnel 
Using the Perspective connector, build me a complete lead funnel for [your offer, e.g. a free AI tool pack].
1. Build a lead-magnet landing page: write the hook, the supporting copy, the form fields, and the layout. Make it mobile-first and on-brand for [your niche / audience].
2. Write a 5-email follow-up sequence. Email 1 delivers the resource, emails 2 to 4 build trust with quick wins and proof, email 5 makes a soft pitch to [your paid offer or next step]. Keep my voice warm and human, no hype.
3. Connect the page to my contact list so new leads are captured automatically.
4. Show me a full preview of the page and all 5 emails before anything goes live. Once I approve, publish it to my domain.

how to run it

How to get the most out of it

  1. 🔗 Connect your account and domain first. The publish step only works if Perspective is connected and your domain is set up, so do that before you build, especially if you are filming.
  2. 👀 Always preview before you publish. Treat the first build as 90 percent done. Read the page and every email, then tweak the copy so it sounds like you, not a template.
  3. 🎯 Be specific about the offer and the audience. The vaguer the prompt, the more generic the funnel. Name the offer, who it is for, and the one action you want them to take.
  4. 🪜 It works at every level. Brand new? Paste the mega prompt exactly as-is and let Claude make the calls. More advanced? Feed it your brand voice notes, your existing best-performing email, and your segments, and have it match all three.
the honest bit

The honest bit

The structure and wiring it does for you is the slow part you are saving. The conversion still comes from your offer and your voice, so review every page and email before it goes live.
