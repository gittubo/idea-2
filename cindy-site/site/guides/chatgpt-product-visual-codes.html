Turn on Higgsfield inside ChatGPT

The richer codes (spins, 360s, animations, UGC video) are powered by the official Higgsfield plugin. It takes about a minute to add.

  1. 🧩 Open the Plugin Directory in ChatGPT from the sidebar, or Settings then Plugins. Shortcut: go to higgsfield.ai/mcp and click Add Higgsfield plugin.
  2. ➕ Add the official Higgsfield plugin Find Higgsfield in the directory, open its listing, and click Add or Connect. Add the official one, not a lookalike.
  3. 🔑 Connect your Higgsfield account New to Higgsfield? Make your account at higgsfield.ai first, then sign in and authorize the access (no API key needed). This is the step the quick version skips, so do not miss it or nothing will generate.
  4. 💬 Bring it into a chat Pick Higgsfield from the tools menu or just name it in your message, then paste a code or a prompt below with your product photo.
Honest heads up so nothing surprises you: you need a ChatGPT account and a connected Higgsfield account, and generations run on Higgsfield credits (a paid Higgsfield plan). Still images come from ChatGPT itself, the motion and video come from Higgsfield. Audio and website building are not available inside ChatGPT (use the Higgsfield app for those).
