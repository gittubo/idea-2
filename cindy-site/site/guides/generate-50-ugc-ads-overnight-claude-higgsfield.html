the setup

Connect Higgsfield to Claude (one time)

This is the unlock. You only do it once, then Claude can generate video on its own.

  1. Copy the connector link. Go to higgsfield.ai/mcp and copy the connector link. The link you paste is mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp
  2. Add a custom connector. In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors, then Add custom connector.
  3. Name and paste. Name it Higgsfield, paste the link, then click Add and Connect.
  4. Authorize it. Sign in with your Higgsfield account to authorize it. You need a paid Higgsfield plan (Plus is the minimum for video), so set that up first.
  5. Optional power-up. Upload Higgsfield's skill file so Claude knows exactly how to drive the engine. Get it at github.com/higgsfield-ai/skills

The exact Claude prompt

Drop in about 5 reference photos of the product, plus a couple of screenshots of the brand's best ads, then paste this. Claude studies what works and hands back fifty concepts, then drives Higgsfield to render them.

🧠 the creative director prompt 
You are my performance creative director. I am giving you reference photos of a product and a few examples of the brand's best ads. Your job is to plan fifty distinct UGC-style video ad concepts and then generate them with the Higgsfield connector.

Product: [name and one line on what it is]
Who it is for: [target customer]
The angle or offer: [the benefit, problem it solves, or promo]

First, study the reference ads and tell me the 3 patterns that make them work (hook style, format, emotional angle).

Then give me fifty ad concepts in a table, each with: the hook line (first 3 seconds), the format (talking head, unboxing, before and after, street interview, problem to solution), the actor type, and the on-screen text. Vary the hooks, angles, and actors so I can test widely.

Then, using the Higgsfield connector, generate the first ten as UGC-style videos so I can review the quality. Once I approve, keep going in batches of ten until all fifty are done, and save them so I can find them in the morning.
the flow

Generate fifty ads overnight, step by step

  1. Connect it once. Connect Higgsfield to Claude once, using the steps above.
  2. Feed it references. Drop in 5 inspo product photos and 2 to 3 of the brand's best ads.
  3. Paste the prompt. Claude finds the winning patterns and writes fifty concepts.
  4. Let it render. Claude calls Higgsfield to render the videos, batch by batch, different actors and hooks each time.
  5. Sleep on it. Set it running before bed. By morning, fifty UGC ads are sitting in your folder, ready to review.
make money from it

How to actually sell these

This is a real service. Small businesses and ecommerce brands need fresh ad creative constantly, and most cannot produce fifty versions a week on their own.

  1. Pick a target. Pick a local business or a small ecommerce brand whose ads look tired.
  2. Send a free sample. Make them a free sample batch of 5 to 10 ads from their product page.
  3. Pitch the package. Pitch it as a creative testing package: dozens of fresh ad variations every week so they can find winners faster.
  4. Price per package. Price it as a package, not per video. Brands already spend real money on ad creative, and AI testing is far cheaper and faster than hiring five creators, so the value is easy to show.

3 bonus prompts to run next

🪝 15 scroll-stopping hooks 
Here is my product: [describe it]. Act as a direct response strategist. Give me 15 scroll-stopping hooks for UGC video ads, mixing curiosity, problem to solution, and bold claim angles. Keep each hook under 8 words.
🔍 reverse-engineer a competitor 
Here are 3 of a competitor's best-performing ads: [paste or describe them]. Break down exactly why each one works, then give me 10 ways to make a better version for my own product.
📊 the media buyer read 
I run a small business that sells [product]. Act as a media buyer. Tell me how many ad variations I should test to find a winner, how to read the results, and which creative to scale, in plain language.
the craft

How to get the most out of it

Feed Claude real reference ads, not just the product photo. The better the examples you give it, the sharper the concepts come back. And always review the first ten before you let it run the full fifty, so you catch the look early and only scale the good ones.
what to watch

The honest part

Volume is not the whole game. A great offer and a real product still matter, and not every batch will have a winner. The big income numbers you see online are real but rare, so treat them as proof it is possible, not the average. The advantage here is speed: you can test more ideas in a night than most teams test in a month.
