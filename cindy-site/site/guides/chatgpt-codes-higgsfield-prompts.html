the four video prompts

The Higgsfield prompts I showed

These need the plugin. Paste the whole block, not just the first line, and attach your product photo.

1 · frozen mid-splash 
Take the attached product and build a single continuous shot of it frozen mid-splash. Liquid is caught at the exact moment of impact, sheets and droplets suspended completely still in the air around it, nothing moving in the liquid at all. The camera is the only thing in motion, orbiting slowly around the product and passing between the suspended droplets so you feel the depth of the scene. Hard key light from behind to catch the edges of every droplet, deep shadow behind, dark seamless backdrop. Keep the product label sharp and readable throughout. One continuous shot.
2 · the ingredient bed 
Top-down shot, camera locked directly overhead and completely still. A hand enters frame and lowers the attached product onto a dense bed of fresh limes, releases it, and withdraws. The product settles into the fruit and stays absolutely locked in position, centred in frame. Then the bed underneath it changes: limes become oranges, oranges become berries, berries become crushed ice, each transition happening cleanly underneath the product while the product itself never moves, never shifts and never changes. Bright even overhead light, rich saturated colour, no visible shadow on the label. One continuous shot.
3 · breaking the frame 
Build this as a phone screen showing an Instagram post of the attached product. Cheese begins to drip out of the photo itself, running over the edge of the post and down across the Instagram interface below it, over the like and comment icons, and pools onto a cartoon character sitting underneath the post who looks up at it. The drip must clearly cross from inside the photo to outside it, so it reads as the food escaping the post. Warm appetising light on the food, flat accurate UI colours, camera locked on the phone screen. One continuous shot.
4 · the sticker peel 
Start on the attached photo as a flat printed image. A hand enters frame, catches the corner of the main subject, and peels it off the photo like a die-cut sticker, lifting it cleanly away from the surface with a thick white sticker border around its edge. The background of the photo stays completely still and unchanged as the subject comes away, leaving the space behind it visible. Soft even light, slight paper curl at the peel edge, camera locked and level. One continuous shot.
💡 One extra prompt worth having. If any of the four comes back flat, paste this straight after and it usually fixes it in one pass:
the fixer 
That was close but it looks generic. Redo it with these specifics: name the exact camera move and its speed, name the key light direction and its hardness, name the backdrop, and keep my product's label sharp and readable in every frame. Change nothing about the concept, only make the camera, lighting and materials specific. Keep it one continuous shot with no cuts.
