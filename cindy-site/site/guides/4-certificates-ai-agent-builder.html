4 certificates to become an AI agent builder

Hey, it's Cindy 🌱 Here are all four certificates from the reel, with the direct link, what each one costs, how long it takes and what it actually tests.

the short version

The whole thing in 4 steps

  1. ⚡ Start with Databricks for the vocabulary. Ninety minutes, four videos, one quiz. It gives you the words the other three assume you already know.
  2. 🔌 Do the Hugging Face MCP course next. Free, two certificates, and it is the one that teaches the protocol agents actually use to reach tools.
  3. 🏛 Then sit Oracle's free exam. The only real certification exam on this list that costs nothing right now. About six and a half hours of prep, then sixty minutes.
  4. 🎓 Finish with LangChain if you want the paid credential. The prep courses are free, so you can do all the learning first and decide about the $99 exam later.
You do not have to do all four. If you only do two, do Databricks and Hugging Face. If you want something with a formal exam on your profile, add Oracle, because it is free.
why this order

Where this path comes from

LinkedIn's 2026 Jobs on the Rise list for the US puts artificial intelligence engineers at number one, and the "most common skills" line for the role reads "LangChain, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), PyTorch" (source). LangChain being listed first is why this path ends there rather than starting there. The three before it exist so that when you sit the LangChain exam you are not learning what an agent is at the same time as learning their framework.

all four links

1. Databricks AI Agent Fundamentals

Open the training · 90 minutes · free

What it is. Four short videos covering the foundational concepts of AI agents, then a quiz. The page is labelled Free Training and says "Learn foundational concepts of AI agents in 90 minutes."

What it actually tests. Whether you understand what an agent is as a system: how it reasons, what it means for it to take an action, and where an agent belongs versus a fixed script. Concepts rather than code.

What you walk away with. A Databricks badge when you pass the quiz. Their page adds "Add the badge to your LinkedIn profile", which tells you exactly what it is for.

Do this one on a weeknight. It is the shortest thing on the list and it makes the other three noticeably easier, because you stop having to look up terminology mid-lesson.

2. Hugging Face MCP course

Open the course · free, including certification · two certificates

What it is. A free course built in partnership with Anthropic on the Model Context Protocol, which is the standard agents use to connect to tools and data. If Databricks teaches you what an agent is, this teaches you how one actually reaches the outside world.

What it actually tests. Unit 1 covers the fundamentals and has its own certificate exam. Units 2 and 3 are the use-case units, and finishing those earns the second one.

What you walk away with. Two separate credentials from one course: a Fundamentals certificate after Unit 1, and a Certificate of Completion after Units 2 and 3. Their wording: "The certification process is completely free."

The honest bit. The prerequisites list "Experience with at least one programming language (Python or TypeScript examples will be shown)", plus basic understanding of LLM concepts and familiarity with API concepts. The page says these are not strictly required and links supplementary resources, but a little Python makes this a much better afternoon.

3. Oracle Agentic AI Foundations Associate

Open the learning path · about 6.5 hours of course · 60-minute exam, free

What it is. The only item here that is a proper vendor certification with an exam code (1Z0-1157-26), and Oracle is currently running it at no cost. The learning path bundles the training and the exam together: "6+ Hours of expert training", a main course of 6h 28m, a short "Prepare for Certification" section, an exam topics breakdown and a practice exam.

What it actually tests. Oracle University's announcement says the exam is "40 questions to be answered within 60 minutes, with a passing score of 65%" (source). It covers agentic AI concepts and how they run on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.

What you walk away with. An actual Oracle certification, the kind that normally carries a fee, plus a badge you can verify.

The honest bit. The stated prerequisites are "Basic familiarity with LLM concepts. Functional knowledge of Python. Basic familiarity with Oracle Cloud Infrastructure." This is the most technical of the free three, and the OCI-specific parts mean some of what you learn is Oracle's way of doing things rather than universal.

This is the one to do first if you only do one. A free vendor certification exam is unusual and it is the kind of thing that stops being free without warning. Oracle has not announced an end date, but the whole reason this is worth telling people about is that right now it costs nothing.

4. LangChain Certified Agent Engineer

Open the certification page · $99 USD exam · prep courses free

What it is. The paid one, and the one that maps directly onto the skill LinkedIn lists first for AI engineers. Five foundational prep courses on LangChain Academy are marked Free, so all the learning costs nothing and only the exam is paid.

What it actually tests. The page lists 40 multiple-choice questions with 28 out of 40 to pass, 120 minutes (about 135 minutes seat time), online proctored. The credential is valid for 24 months.

What you walk away with. A proctored, time-limited credential in the framework the role is hiring for. Of everything here, this is the one that looks most like a professional certification rather than a course badge.

Do the free prep courses even if you never sit the exam. They are the same material, they cost nothing, and you can decide about the $99 once you know whether you would pass.
what this costs

The real total

Three of the four are free end to end, including certification. The only money on this list is the LangChain exam.

Free path Databricks, Hugging Face and Oracle. Roughly 9 hours of study, three credentials including one real vendor certification, $0.
Full path All four, adding the LangChain exam at $99 USD. About 11 hours plus exam time.
after the badges

Making them count

  1. 🔨 Build one agent that does something real. The Hugging Face course leaves you able to connect an agent to an actual tool. Do that for a job you personally repeat, and record thirty seconds of it working.
  2. 📌 List them properly. LinkedIn's Licenses and certifications section, using each credential's verification URL. Oracle's and LangChain's are the two worth leading with, because both come with real exams.
  3. 🗣 Be accurate about what each one is. Oracle and LangChain are certifications. Databricks is a badge. Hugging Face is two course certificates. Saying that correctly costs you nothing and it is the kind of precision that reads as competence.
