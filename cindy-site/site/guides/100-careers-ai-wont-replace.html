your move

How to use this list

Pick three. Not the three with the biggest paycheck. The three where the underlying skill is one you already lean on without trying. That's your moat. The next 90 days of your career compound when you spend them sharpening one of those, not when you panic-learn another AI tool.

If none of these careers fit, that's fine. The point is the skill, not the title. Find your job's version of taste, charisma, original thinking. Lean there.
