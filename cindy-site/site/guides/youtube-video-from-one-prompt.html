the workflow

One prompt to a finished video

The trick most people miss: the tool is only as good as the brief you feed it. So you write a sharp brief with Claude, then let the video tool do the production.

  1. 🧠 Write the brief with Claude. Don't paste a one-liner. Have Claude turn your rough idea into a proper video brief: title, angle, target length, tone, hook, and a section-by-section outline. This is the step that decides whether the finished video is good or generic.
  2. 📋 Paste it into VidRush. Drop that brief into VidRush and hit generate. From that one input it runs the whole production line: research, script, AI voiceover, footage, editing, and a thumbnail.
  3. ⏳ Wait a few minutes, then download. A short queue and you get a finished long-form video (VidRush does 6 to 40 minutes) plus an auto-generated thumbnail. Download it and upload straight to your channel.

the honest bit

What it really costs, and the free alternative

The reel makes it look free. It isn't. Here's the real picture, plus three tools that do the same one-prompt-to-video job, one of which has a genuine free plan.

VidRush, the paid pick The one from the reel. Best-in-class one-prompt long-form (6 to 40 min). $99/mo minimum, no free trial, currently waitlist. vidrush.ai
InVideo AI, the free alternative The closest free match. One prompt to a full video with script, AI voiceover and footage, up to around 30 min. Real free plan (720p, watermark). invideo.io
Pictory Script-to-video, and it can turn a blog post, doc, or URL into a narrated video. Free trial to test it first. pictory.ai
Fliki Paste a script or one-line prompt and it adds AI voiceover, visuals, music and subtitles. Free plan, no card needed. The lightest of the three for long-form. fliki.ai
What actually makes these work is the topic and the brief, not the tool. Faceless "top 10", "rarest", and "the history of" formats perform because they're search-friendly and easy to script. Nail the brief with Claude and any of these will give you something publishable. Start free with InVideo AI, and only upgrade to VidRush once the channel is genuinely earning.
