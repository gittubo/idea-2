The 30-second prompt that actually shrinks your to-do list

Your to-do list never shrinks because everything on it feels important. So you keep adding and never subtracting, and the list quietly becomes a guilt pile instead of a plan.

This one prompt fixes that in about 30 seconds. It runs your brain-dump through the Eisenhower matrix, urgent versus important, and sorts every task into do now, schedule, delegate, or delete. The magic is the delete bucket and the pushback: Claude will tell you what to kill, which is the exact part you can't do for yourself. Paste it, dump your list, and get back a clean plan.

the four buckets

What this does

The Eisenhower matrix is a simple grid: every task is either urgent or not, important or not. That gives you four buckets, and most of your stress lives in the two you're avoiding.

  1. 🔥 Do now is urgent and important. The real fires.
  2. 🗓️ Schedule is important but not urgent. The stuff that actually moves your life forward, which is why it keeps getting skipped.
  3. 🤝 Delegate is urgent but not important. Hand it off or automate it.
  4. 🗑️ Delete is neither. The hardest bucket, because you have to admit it doesn't matter.

The mega prompt

Paste this into Claude, then dump your entire task list underneath it. Don't edit or pre-sort, that's Claude's job.

🗂️ The Eisenhower sorter 
You are my Eisenhower sorter. I'm going to dump my entire task list, unfiltered. Sort every item into four buckets: do now (urgent and important), schedule (important, not urgent), delegate (urgent, not important), and delete (neither). Push back on me, because most things I think are important actually aren't, and be honest about which ones to kill and why. For anything in delegate, suggest who or what could take it. How to get the most out of it

  1. 🧠 Dump everything, no editing. The value comes from seeing the whole pile at once. If you pre-filter, you've just done the sorting badly yourself.
  2. 🗑️ Let it cut. The delete bucket is the whole point. If you argue with every deletion, you're back to treating everything as important.
  3. 🪜 It works at every level. New to this? Paste the prompt exactly as-is. More advanced? Save it in your Claude settings instructions, then add a line about your role and goals so it sorts against what actually matters to you.
  4. 🔁 Re-run it weekly. A sorted list decays fast. Make it a Monday-morning ritual with the weekly-review prompt above.

The honest bit

Claude doesn't know your life, so its first sort is a strong starting point, not the final word. Use the pushback to make your own call, especially on the delete and delegate buckets. The point isn't to obey it, it's to be forced to defend why something is really on your list.
