Design posters like a world-class designer (Reve reference trick)

Hey, it's Cindy 🌱 This is the reference trick real designers use: feed Reve a look you already love so it borrows the art direction (not copies it) and builds your poster in 4K.

Most AI posters look generic because everyone types the same vague prompt and hopes. The trick real designers use is to feed the AI a reference, a look you already love, and tell it to borrow the art direction, not copy it. Reve does this beautifully, builds the whole thing in 4K, then lets you fix any single word or colour by drawing right on the poster.

the four moves

What this does

  1. 🖼️ Designs from a reference. Drop in a poster whose style you love and Reve uses it as art direction, the typography, layout, palette, and energy, then builds your poster around your own topic.
  2. 🏆 Renders in 4K. You get a high-end, designed-looking poster in one pass, not a flat template.
  3. ✏️ Fixes one thing without re-rolling. If a headline comes out wrong, draw on it, tap that text layer, and rewrite just that word. Nothing else moves.
  4. 🎨 Every object is a layer. Select the background and recolour only that, or grab and resize any element.
the build

How to do it

  1. 🌐 Open Reve. Type reve.com, press Go to app, then Build from scratch.
  2. 🖼️ Drop in your reference. Add a poster whose style you want to borrow. Use your own design or a license-free one, never a real brand's artwork.
  3. 📋 Paste the designer prompt below and swap in what you are building.
  4. 🖊️ Refine on the poster. Draw on any wrong text and rewrite that layer, recolour the background, resize objects, then zoom in to check the 4K detail.

The world-class designer prompt

📋 the art direction prompt 
You are a world-class designer. Use the attached design as a visual reference to guide the art direction, typography, composition, colour palette, texture, layout style, and overall visual energy, but only as inspiration, for [describe what you are building, e.g. a cream and coral skincare launch poster].
[attach the design whose style you love]
the craft

How to get the most out of it

  1. 🎯 Pick a strong reference. The better the look you feed it, the better it designs. Choose one with clear typography and a palette you love.
  2. ✍️ Be specific about your topic. Name the product, the vibe, and any text you want on it.
  3. 🖊️ Edit, don't re-roll. Fix the one wrong word or colour with the draw-on-image tools instead of regenerating.
  4. 🔍 Zoom in before you export. Check the small text is sharp, that is your 4K quality proof.
the honest bit

The honest bit

Use a reference you have the right to use, your own work or a license-free design, not a real brand's poster. Reve gives you a high-end first draft, but read the copy and tweak anything that does not sound like you before you post.
